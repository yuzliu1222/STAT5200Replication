# Replication of Ludwig et al. (2021)

STAT 5200 group replication project, University of Pennsylvania, Fall 2026.

We replicate:

> Ludwig, J. O., Davies, N. M., Bor, J., & De Neve, J.-W. (2021). "Causal effect of children's secondary education on parental health outcomes: findings from a natural experiment in Botswana." *BMJ Open*, 11, e043247.

The paper uses Botswana's 1996 education reform as an instrument for children's schooling and finds no effect on parental survival.

## Data

Botswana 2001 and 2011 census samples from [IPUMS International](https://international.ipums.org). Data are not included in this repo at this point.
